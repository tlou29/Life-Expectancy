# 🌍 Life Expectancy Analysis: Global Trends and SADC Region

## 📌 Project Overview

This project investigates how life expectancy varies across countries and over time, focusing on the relationship between life expectancy and key socioeconomic and health indicators.

The analysis examines global patterns and compares them with countries within the **Southern African Development Community (SADC)** region.

It explores how education, adult mortality, and HIV/AIDS indicators are associated with life expectancy using statistical analysis and data exploration.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore global life expectancy trends across countries and years.
- Investigate the relationship between education and life expectancy.
- Examine how adult mortality relates to life expectancy.
- Analyse the association between HIV/AIDS and life expectancy.
- Compare global findings with patterns observed in the SADC region.
- Identify missing values and limitations affecting data reliability.

## 📊 Key Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| Life Expectancy | Average expected lifespan of a population |
| Education | Education-related indicators |
| Adult Mortality | Adult mortality rates |
| HIV/AIDS | HIV/AIDS-related health indicators |
| Country | Country-level observations |
| Year | Year of observation |

## 🔬 Methodology

The project applies the following statistical techniques:

1. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Understanding the dataset and identifying patterns.
2. **Descriptive Statistics:** Summarising the distribution of key variables.
3. **Missing Value Analysis:** Identifying and examining incomplete observations.
4. **Confidence Intervals:** Estimating population parameters using sample data.
5. **Hypothesis Testing:** Investigating statistically significant differences and relationships.
6. **Correlation Analysis:** Measuring the strength and direction of associations between variables.
7. **Regional Comparison:** Comparing global patterns with SADC countries.

## 🌍 Scope of Analysis

The investigation follows two main perspectives:

- **Global Analysis:** Examines life expectancy patterns across countries and over time.
- **SADC Analysis:** Investigates regional patterns and compares them with global observations.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Missing values may affect the reliability of statistical results.
- Country-level averages may hide inequalities within populations.
- Differences in data collection and reporting may affect comparisons.
- Correlation does not imply causation.
- Historical, economic, social, and healthcare differences must be considered when interpreting regional findings.

## 💡 Key Takeaway

Understanding life expectancy requires examining more than one indicator. Education, adult mortality, and HIV/AIDS provide important perspectives on population health, while regional and historical contexts are essential for interpreting differences between countries.
